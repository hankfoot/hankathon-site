A one-day making party
Saturday, September 26
Seattle, WA

overview / logistics / join / showcase

Hi! My name is Hank, and I'm about to turn 30.

I asked my partner for a pug for my birthday, and that idea didn't seem to get much traction. So, in my abundance of jobless free time, I've cooked up an alternative idea: hosting a Hankathon!

+---------------------+

| WHAT'S A HANKATHON? |

+---------------------+

It's a spinoff of a "hackathon (opens in a new tab)," which is an event where people get together to write code for multiple hours straight, cut corners on personal hygiene, and sleep on the floor. Bear with me here, it'll get better.

A Hankathon™ imbues a hackathon with the needs and desires of me (Hank): an aging millennial creative technologist.

Best parts of a hackathon

  • Timeboxed creativity
  • Make cool things with friends
  • Learning about new tools/tech

Hank's needs and desires

  • Unburdening myself from "productive" side projects
  • Going to bed on time
  • Eating snacks that don't make my tummy hurt

Put simply, I want to bring a bunch of people together and give you an excuse to spend the day making fun, weird, silly art+tech projects that otherwise are hard to carve out time for.

+---------------------------+

| SO... WHAT ARE WE MAKING? |

+---------------------------+

I'd love to see beautifully unhinged projects that have never graced this planet before. A massive LED upgrade to your bike? Yes please. A giant Labubu suit? Love it! A fire-shooting twinkie??? Probably not, but love the energy.

There are no hard rules on artistic media, but I'm imagining folks will gravitate towards:

  • Arts, crafts, and arts & crafts
  • Something something vibecoding
  • Gadgets or gizmos

No particular skills are required. If you watch this video (opens in a new tab) and are left thinking, "wow, I wish I could do that," you are in the right place.

+-----------+

| LOGISTICS |

+-----------+

Locations and timing are still TBD, but here's roughly what I'm thinking:

Friday, September 25 · Evening

An optional mixer to meet other makers, brainstorm ideas, and form project teams.

Saturday, September 26 · All day

A full day of making, with access to a large workspace and shared tools.

Also Saturday, September 26 · Evening

An open house to show off the projects, eat, drink, and be merry.

Seattle, downtown-ish area. Exact venue TBD.

If you're not around and still want to participate, jump to participating remotely.

+--------------------+

| JOIN THE HANKATHON |

+--------------------+

If this sounds like a fun way to spend a Saturday, sign up here (opens in a new tab). It's FREE!

Once you register, I'll add you to a Discord server where we'll share more details about what to bring and all that (maybe there'll even be a theme 🤷‍♀️).

Space is limited to 30 people, so please keep your RSVP updated if something comes up. There's a waitlist feature and it should ping the next person automatically if a spot opens.

If you know someone who'd be interested in this, PLEASE INVITE THEM! I'd love to better foster this sort of community in Seattle.

+-----------------------+

| COME FOR THE SHOWCASE |

+-----------------------+

On the evening of the 26th, everything made during the Hankathon goes on display. Come see the finished projects, meet the people who made them, and stay for food, drinks, and a celebration of art+tech weirdness.

This event is open to all! Participation in the Hankathon is not required.

RSVP here (opens in a new tab) for timing and venue details.

+----------------------+

| PARTICIPATE REMOTELY |

+----------------------+

Why should Seattlites have all the fun?? If you live elsewhere and are looking for a reason to spend Saturday, September 26 creatively recharging, let's make it happen.

Send me your contact info via this form (opens in a new tab) and I'll shoot out an email closer to the event once I figure out how this could work. I'm imagining we could gather up projects digitally (photos, websites, etc.) and show them at the showcase.
